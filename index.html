The Sunflower
Across Universes

They say that once every year, the universes briefly touch. Not because of fate. Not because of war. But because somewhere, the sunflower blooms again.

And in every universe, the sunflower is still you.

Happy Birthday, Amisha

Universe One
The Golden City

In this world, sunflowers bloom wherever a kind soul walks. So when you arrive, the streets turn gold and even ordinary evenings become celebrations.

“Today, the whole city blooms for the day its sunflower was born.”

Universe Two
Neon Dreams

Nature disappeared long ago. But one glowing sunflower still grows beneath the neon skyline. It leads me to you again, walking beside me through a future that still remembers your birthday.

“Birthday protocol activated: celebrate the sunflower in every timeline.”

Universe Three
The Kingdom of Light

Every year, lanterns rise when the legendary sunflower blooms. This year, they rise because you are here — and the kingdom is warmer simply because you exist.

“The brightest soul deserves the brightest celebration.”

Universe Four
Moonlit Tides

Here, the tide rises once a year to greet the sunflower. We walk along the shore hand in hand, while the ocean remembers the day you were born.

“Even the waves know that tonight belongs to you.”

Universe Five
The Cosmic Garden

I searched through galaxies and impossible worlds. Still, every constellation pointed in the same direction: toward you, the sunflower the universe never forgets.

“Even the stars celebrate the day their sunflower was born.”

The Real Universe

After seeing all of these worlds, I understood something: the sunflower was never magic. It was simply the way you make life feel — warmer, safer, brighter.

And out of every possible universe, this one is my favorite. Because this is the one where I get to love you.

Across Every Universe...

If there are infinite universes, then there are infinite versions of me trying to make you smile today.

And in every single one, you are still the sunflower.

Happy Birthday, my sunflower.
I would still choose you in every universe.
